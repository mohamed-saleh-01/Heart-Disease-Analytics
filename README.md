## 📌 Project Overview
This end-to-end Data Science project explores the intersection of lifestyle, demographics, and cardiovascular health. Using a dataset of over 300K+ records, I performed deep exploratory data analysis (EDA), applied rigorous data cleaning, and engineered a machine learning pipeline to predict heart disease with 91% accuracy. The final solution is delivered through a professional multi-page Power BI dashboard for actionable medical insights.

## 📊 Key Insights Discovered
* Age Factor: Heart disease risk increases significantly after the age of 55, peaking in the 65-69 bracket.
* BMI Impact: Overweight and Obese categories show a much higher prevalence of heart disease.
* Lifestyle Influence: Factors like smoking, alcohol consumption, and lack of sleep show strong correlations with heart health.
* General Health: Self-reported "Poor" or "Fair" health status is a strong indicator of cardiovascular issues.

---

## 🤖 Machine Learning Modeling
Tested multiple classification algorithms to ensure the highest precision for medical prediction:
| Algorithm | Accuracy | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Logistic Regression | 91% | Baseline performance & interpretability. |
| Decision Tree | 91% | Mapping complex non-linear patient features. |
| Random Forest | 89.7% | Ensemble learning to reduce variance. |

*Pre-processing steps included handling missing values, feature encoding, and standard scaling to ensure model reliability.*

---

## 💡 Key Findings & Conclusions
* Age & BMI: These are the most significant non-modifiable and modifiable risk factors.
* Lifestyle Impact: Smoking and lack of sleep show a clear correlation with heart issues.
* Model Reliability: With 91% accuracy, the Logistic Regression model can be a valuable tool for preliminary screening.
